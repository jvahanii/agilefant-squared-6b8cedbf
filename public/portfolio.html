    Agilefant² The free backlog tool that scales, rebuilt from the ground up.

    Agilefant is back as a modern web app. It scales from one person's to-do list to an organisation's whole portfolio, and pulls work in from the tools teams already use.

    • Lists nest as deep as the work needs, one item can sit on several lists, and nearly everything works from the keyboard.
    • Two-way Jira sync for statuses, descriptions and web links, including edits and removals.
    • Captures work from Gmail, WhatsApp messages and merged GitHub pull requests, plus a browser extension.
    • Lists can be shared with other organisations or the public, and copied into a new account at sign-up.

    Talentloom Better PeopleOps for everyone.

    Free PeopleOps software that covers the whole path from finding a role to growing, organising and paying the people who fill it. Sign-in is by emailed code, with no passwords, and the job board, applications and survey links work without an account.

    Hiring
    A public job board with ads imported every morning, applications without an account, a candidate flow board from Applied to Hired, analytics and CSV import.
    Skills
    Who knows what and how well, on 2–7 named levels. Strategic skills, development charts over time, and suggestions to merge skills written two ways.
    Learning together
    Drag one name onto another to pair people up to learn a skill, and see who is actively levelling up.
    Org chart & teams
    Drag-and-drop org charts, planned and past versions compared side by side, supervisor suggestions, teams of teams, and an Org Topologies map.
    Career paths
    A career map of job families and levels. Pick a person to see which roles they are Ready, Close or Further from.
    Compensation
    Salary history, salary costs per unit on the org chart, bulk raises previewed before they apply, and bonus schemes with thresholds, caps and payouts.
    Work & vacations
    A shared absence calendar with national holidays, vacation days earned and left, custom absence kinds, and a change history that can't be edited.
    Pulse
    Free surveys and structured interviews run in rounds. Personal links follow each person's answers over time and compare every round.

    Next: Talentloom IQ, the reasoning test trainer, is being rebuilt into Talentloom in a CollabAI workshop, December 2026.

    It started as a recruiting pipeline tracker. Each organisation's data is kept apart by row-level security in Postgres.

    Piharadikaali Finds, reviews and reframes skate tricks from 360° footage.

    Point it at a folder of GoPro MAX clips and it finds every likely trick, builds picture sheets for a quick review, and renders each keeper as a short 16:9 clip that follows the rider. It runs on your own PC and graphics card, with no internet or AI service.

    • Scans clips while they are still copying off the camera.
    • Each attempt is judged as landed, fall or fail, and only the keepers get rendered.
    • Clip endings follow a rule fitted to hand trims, landing within 0.6 s of the manual cut on average.
    133possible tricks flagged in one skate session→ 27clips kept after review: 25 landed tricks and 2 falls, every one worth keeping

    Talentloom IQ A reasoning test trainer that works offline.

    Practise the kinds of puzzles used in aptitude tests: number series, letter series, mixed number-and-letter series, pattern completion and winding shape sequences. Every task is generated in the browser from rules, nothing is sent anywhere, and it works without a network.

    • Three difficulty levels or a random mix, and 1–5 items to continue in each series.
    • An optional time limit per task (15–90 s) or for the whole set (7 or 10 minutes).
    • Statistics by puzzle type, kept in your browser.

    Next: being rebuilt as part of Talentloom in a CollabAI workshop, December 2026.

    Keywi Plan and track savings and income together.

    Built with a team of 14–17-year-old summer trainees, as a better replacement for Boston Consulting Group's Key Impact Tracking tool. A shared savings and income tracker that works for one person, a household or a large organisation. Members join a workspace with an 8-character code and follow goals, monthly entries and progress charts together.

    • Multiple workspaces per person, with admin and member roles and an audit log.
    • Show figures in any currency; values are stored in euros and converted for display.
    • Finnish and English interface, Google sign-in, and export and import for admins.
